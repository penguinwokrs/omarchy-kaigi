Kaigi

Your next meeting and a countdown in the Omarchy bar. Join with one click, get notified when meetings start and end, and optionally have the meeting open by itself.

What it does

  • Shows the next meeting and the time until it starts (or the time left) in the bar.
  • Lists today's (and optionally tomorrow's) events; click one to open its Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or other meeting link.
  • Sends a notification when a meeting starts and ends, and can open the meeting link automatically.
  • Reads your calendars from Google Calendar (sign in with Google, read-only) or from iCalendar feeds.

How Kaigi uses your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Kaigi asks for read-only access to your calendar events and your calendar list. It uses them only to show your upcoming meetings on your own computer. Kaigi runs entirely on your machine; it has no server and sends your data nowhere except to Google's own API. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Install

Kaigi is a plugin for the Omarchy Linux desktop (Omarchy 4.5 or later):

omarchy plugin add https://github.com/penguinwokrs/omarchy-kaigi --enable

Then open the Kaigi popup in the bar and press Sign in with Google.

Source and support

Kaigi is open source (Apache-2.0): github.com/penguinwokrs/omarchy-kaigi. Report problems in its issue tracker.
