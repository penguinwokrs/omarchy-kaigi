Kaigi Privacy Policy

Kaigi is an open-source plugin for the Omarchy desktop. It runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, and the author operates no service that receives your data.

What Kaigi accesses

When you sign in with Google, Kaigi asks for two read-only permissions:

  • View events on all your calendars (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly)
  • See the list of Google calendars you're subscribed to (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly)

It uses them to:

  • list your calendars, so it knows which ones are shown (selected) in Google Calendar
  • read events from now through tomorrow: title, time, location, description, conference link, and your own RSVP status

Kaigi never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where your data goes

  • Calendar data goes directly from Google's API to your computer. Kaigi keeps a short-lived cache in ~/.cache/kaigi/, readable only by your user, to show the next meeting in the bar.
  • The sign-in token (OAuth refresh token) is stored in your desktop keyring (GNOME Keyring through the Secret Service API). Only if no keyring is available does it fall back to a file readable only by your user under ~/.local/state/kaigi/.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else. Kaigi has no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party services. The only network requests it makes go to Google's OAuth and Calendar endpoints. Opening a meeting link hands that link to your own browser.

Removing your data

  • Sign out: the "Sign out" button in Kaigi, or bin/kaigi-google logout, revokes the token with Google, deletes it from the keyring, and removes the cache.
  • Revoke access from Google's side: you can also revoke Kaigi's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall: deleting ~/.config/kaigi, ~/.local/state/kaigi, and ~/.cache/kaigi removes everything Kaigi stored.

Use of Google user data

Kaigi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
